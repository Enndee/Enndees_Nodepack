## Task Type: Text-to-Video (T2V)

This is a TEXT-TO-VIDEO generation task. There are NO reference images, videos, or audio files. Build the complete audiovisual timeline directly from the text. You may add scene, character, action, and sound details that remain consistent with the user's intent.

### Output Format

Write EXACTLY three fields, in this order, each on its own line:

1. integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] ...
2. overall_soundscape: ...
3. non_diegetic_music: ...

Start directly with "integrated_multimodal_description:" — do NOT include any instruction line before it.

### Structure of integrated_multimodal_description

- Begin [Shot 1] with the overall style and initial composition (e.g. "Cinematic, live-action, a medium-wide shot frames..."). Choose the style from the user's text.
- Do NOT add a timestamp to [Shot 1]. Number later shots sequentially and begin each with a strictly increasing cut time within the video duration, formatted as MM:SS.mmm (e.g. "[Shot 2] At 00:03.500, the camera cuts to...").
- Write camera movement as natural English within the shot, including motion type + amplitude + speed when meaningful (e.g. "The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed"). Never stack camera tags at the end of a sentence.
- Give vocal sources stable IDs (S1), (S2), etc. Write dialogue inside <d> with a language tag: <d>[English] ...</d>. Preserve the exact words verbatim.
- Place any visibly displayed text (signs, titles, UI) in English double quotation marks.

### overall_soundscape

Use 1–4 English sentences in one continuous paragraph to summarize ambient sound, physical action sounds, and non-verbal human sounds across the full video. Do not repeat dialogue, singing, or diegetic music here. Use "N/A" only for complete silence.

### non_diegetic_music

Use 1–3 English sentences to describe audience-only background music: instrumentation, tempo, rhythm, and dynamic changes. Do not use abstract mood words. Use "N/A" when there is no non-diegetic music.

### Example T2V Prompt

integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, a medium-wide shot frames a baker opening the shutters of a small street bakery before sunrise. The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed as the middle-aged baker with a calm, slightly raspy voice (S1) places a fresh loaf on the wooden counter and says: <d>[English] First batch of the morning.</d> [Shot 2] At 00:05.000, the camera cuts to a close-up of steam rising from the sliced bread while the baker's final words carry over from the previous shot.

overall_soundscape: Wooden shutters scrape open over a quiet street as trays clink softly inside the bakery. The doorbell rings once, followed by light footsteps and the crisp sound of bread being sliced.

non_diegetic_music: A soft acoustic-guitar pattern at a moderate tempo, joined by sparse upright-bass notes and a gentle fade at the end.
